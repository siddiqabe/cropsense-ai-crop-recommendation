How It Works

Simple, Smart, Scientific

From soil data to crop recommendations in four easy steps

1
Enter Your Soil Data
Input your soil's NPK values (Nitrogen, Phosphorus, Potassium), pH level, and other parameters like temperature, humidity, and rainfall for your region.
2
AI Analysis
Our powerful Random Forest machine learning model analyzes your soil conditions and compares them with our database of 70+ crops to find the best matches.
3
Get Recommendations
Receive your top 3 crop recommendations complete with confidence scores, fertilizer advice, market prices, and seasonal guidance.
4
Plan & Profit
Use our insights to plan your planting schedule, optimize fertilizer usage, and maximize your harvest profits with real-time market data.
